The Academy

How a spell is cast

Core entries

Every spell reads seven timeframes, from minutes to hours, and grades every setup it finds. Only the highest grade becomes a trade, and only once the candle has closed.

Max: trend add-ons

When the day's trend is clear, Max adds smaller follow-up trades on breakouts and continuations, at a quarter or half of the core risk.

Prime: the second trade

On the very best setups Prime opens a second trade with a tight stop and a long target. At most six open, and none for the rest of the day after five losses.

Counter: the Chameleon

Half size when the daily trend is weak, an extra volatility-sized trade only when it is strong. Built for choppy weeks.
