ALL EAs

Core entries

The EA reads seven timeframes, from minutes to hours, and grades every setup it finds. Only the highest grade becomes a trade, and only once the candle has closed. Each profile fixes which setting it trades and how far its stop and target sit.

MAX

Trend add-ons

When the day's trend is clear, Max adds smaller follow-up trades on breakouts and continuations, sized at a quarter or half of the core risk, so a strong trend pays more without a bigger stop.

PRIME

The second, high-grade trade

On the very best setups Prime opens a second trade with a tight stop and a long target, around four times the risk. It has its own brakes: at most six open, and none for the rest of the day after five losses.

COUNTER

Size that follows the trend

Counter profiles halve their size when the daily trend is weak, and add a volatility-sized trade only when it is strong. Built for choppy weeks.

How it was tested

Tested the hard way

The setup

  • Start balance $100,000
  • Fixed $500 risk per core trade (0.5%), add-ons at their share
  • No compounding, so every month is on the same scale
  • Gold only, every minute with bid and ask

The costs

  • Buys fill at the ask, sells at the bid: the real spread of every minute
  • Plus $0.30 extra slippage on every trade
  • If stop and target could both hit in one minute, the stop counts
  • The EA's own limits applied in time order

The proof

  • Rules built on the recent data; the older part was run afterwards, and all 39 main profiles made money on it
  • Price feed checked against a live broker's own ticks
  • No single trade ever lost more than one stop

The honest part

  • Profiles were chosen on this same history, so these are best-case figures
  • Live trading adds outages, requotes and nerves
  • Every EA runs free on demo: watch it before going live
